# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

- Be welcoming, patient, and respectful — especially toward new contributors and learners
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully; focus on what is best for the community
- No harassment, trolling, insulting comments, or unwelcome attention of any kind

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to **theanilpaneru@gmail.com**. Maintainers will review and take appropriate action, up to and including temporary or permanent bans. All reports will be kept confidential.
